White balance

Your whole keyboard is lit white. Watch the keyboard (not the screen) and drag until it looks neutral. Teal? Lower Green & Blue. Pink or red? Lower Red, or raise the others. A balanced white looks a little dimmer — that's normal, since white can only be as bright as the weakest channel.
